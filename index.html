Hi, I’m Nithin Steven F, a passionate WebXR developer and Solutions Architect with a deep love for immersive technologies. With a background in electrical engineering and extensive experience in both game development and cloud solutions, I specialize in crafting high-performance, engaging XR experiences for the web. My journey in the world of WebXR has been driven by the vision of making the metaverse more accessible, functional, and exciting for users across various platforms. At the heart of my work is Wonderland Engine, where I focus on building optimized, cutting-edge solutions for AR and VR environments. Whether it’s creating realistic terrain, immersive water shaders, or efficient multiplayer experiences, I’m always looking for ways to push the boundaries of what’s possible in WebXR. When I’m not coding, you’ll find me playing guitar or involved in music projects, where I channel my creativity in other ways. Beyond technology, I believe in the fusion of creativity and innovation—bringing storytelling, music, and design into immersive spaces that captivate and inspire. Chroma: The Last Unicorn — My JS13k Journey

✍️ By Nithin Steven 📅 September 13, 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read

✨ Taking on the JS13k Challenge

I always wanted to try the JS13k Games challenge... Now I am so happy to finish and submit it!

This time, I wanted to try something unique: tell a story via the game.

📖 Synopsis

We walk into a dream of a developer who lost his wife in an accident by the cliff. She was his love, but then she sacrificed herself to save him off from a cliff and fell into the grassy valley below.

This incident haunts him and makes his world grey and lifeless. Maya, his wife, guides him in a dream, letting him bring back the colors from the rainbow to his life and helping him see colors again.

🌈 The Motif of the Game The motif of the game is to give a message that no matter how grief-filled a problem or memory is in your life, you can still find hope, bring back your imagination (like a flying unicorn or a fantasy world), and it will bring back your colors and find peace.
"I didn't save you from that cliff for you to live in grey. Stop drowning in deadlines. Chase fireflies. When it rains, find me in the rainbow. Live with color again, my love... I'm always in your mind palace. 🌸🌈"
— Maya's Final Awakening Note

🌿 Lush Environment & Procedural Grass

For this game, I wanted a lush environment that speaks of the valley she fell into...

I always liked the idea of procedural content creation, and was always stunned with rendering grass on the web for almost half a decade. Learned a few things or so with Wonderland Engine as a framework (link to my 1 million grass web showcase), and I was able to use those strategies here too: instancing, resampling, and blending to terrain normals.

🦄 Primitives, Shaders & Fog Cards

I made the unicorn with primitive shapes, and focused heavily on custom shaders for rocks, prisms, and crystal surfaces to meet the artistic vision I had in my mind.

To give a sense of depth, I added fog cards that face the player, and kept the mechanics simple to focus directly on storytelling.

🎨 The 7 Chromatic Tasks

🔴 1. Red — Rain & Code "You stared at code until eyes burned." Catch 3 sparks to realize work cannot replace living.
🟠 2. Orange — Fireflies "Catch them with your hands, silly!" Rediscover childhood laughter and spontaneous joy.
🟡 3. Yellow — Voicemail "Her voice cuts through the cold silence." Reconnecting with the warmth of love left behind.
🟢 4. Green — Sky "Not down where I fell. Look to stars." Breaking free from downward regret and gazing upwards.
🔵 5. Blue — Stillness "When grief chokes you... be still." 20 seconds of physical stillness and centered breathing.
🟣 6. Indigo — Forgiveness "Car skidded. I pushed you clear. Forgive yourself." Releasing survivor's guilt on the cliff.
🦄 7. Violet — Flying Unicorn "What if our horse could fly?" Mount the unicorn as the world erupts into full chromatic radiance.

⚡ Sub-13KB Optimization & AI Acceleration

The main hurdle was fitting everything within the strict 13KB limit. I used Roadroller and Terser to pack the bundle.

And of course, I used Gemini Flash models to speed me up in this process of automation, scripts, and test writings. In these times, not using AI to better use your time would be a waste in my opinion!
